# Cross-layer separation before T1

## Boundary

Current runtime: finite count-based canonical diagnostics through explicit reviewed
and retained H; independently, GameAI-local behavior and derived expression.
No runtime semantics or authority cutover is introduced by this contract.

| Owner | State / authority |
|---|---|
| Godot | World resolution and movement_scale; bounded body snapshots |
| InteractionHistory | Reported approach results, max 128 admissions per process |
| HistoryInfluencePolicy | Opt-in local action influence; fixed 1/3/5-tick profiles |
| Expression | Display projection after action selection, with source IDs |
| Canonical sidecar | Selected counts, frozen M_B, F/F', E; no action authority |
| Assessment ledger | Explicit finite residual review and retained H; no action authority |

Layer containers and owner modules are not canonical M_B fields by identity.
However, finite current relations and parameters sourced from these layers may
constitute M_B when explicitly admitted under a declared model/context boundary.
They are semantically eligible but currently not admitted to the canonical
runtime M_B. Raw E remains
`E-only-not-reviewed`; reviews live in a separate record. Pending H=0 does not
prove that a discrepancy is resolved. Retention does not imply restart durability.

## Acceptance

1. Holding selected counts, observation identity, and finite context fixed, vary
   empty/progress/no-progress history, all three profiles, and movement scale
   0/0.5/1 (27 combinations). Local actions/expressions may differ; canonical
   snapshots and frozen models must remain equal, with no residual H created.
2. Explicit review may change H and retained H, but not the frozen model, raw E,
   history, action, or expression under otherwise unchanged local conditions.
   Recompute with a fresh policy to exclude replay-cache masking.
3. A later change to a selected observation count must still form E under the
   same frozen model. Separation does not mean local actions cannot affect future
   world state and thus future observations/E. Nonzero E alone does not create H.

Evidence: `tests/test_cross_layer_separation.py`. The third case is a selected-
observation fixture, not new live-world evidence; existing Godot/HTTP tests cover
actual action/world/subsequent-observation paths separately.

## Stop boundary

No theta, M_delta, T1 Probe/formation/reconstruction, configurable rho_B, or
canonical action authority is implemented. Before formation work, declare its
finite trigger, candidate model/context identity, selection criteria, and fresh
re-entry acceptance. Psychological affect, social relation meaning, learned
sensitivity, and DNA remain outside this acceptance.

This acceptance proves that current local changes do not silently mutate the
frozen canonical M_B. It does not prove that FoodNeed, neural/body relations, or
learned relations can never participate in a future explicitly formed M_B.
